# Sources, provenance and image limitations

Archive date: 2026-09-06.

## Generated concept images

The eight original PNG files in [images](images/) were generated in this conversation with the
built-in image-generation tool at the user's request. English text was explicitly requested.
Original pixels are preserved; no recompression or redraw was performed for this archive.
Dimensions and SHA-256/Git blob SHA-1 values are recorded in [asset-manifest.json](asset-manifest.json).

These are synthetic illustrations, not photos of a built prototype, users, experiments or
supplier products. Faces, cats, logos/slogans, screens and depicted hardware are invented visual
elements. They do not depict Koala, Harry's mother, Frank or Harry.

Specific limitations observed in the generated outputs:

- Some posters add decorative slogans/branding beyond the requested copy.
- The learning-roadmap poster contains invented terminal output and checked boxes.
  None is a command transcript, sensor result or test verdict.
- The spherical cutaway introduces a dog and a large apparent scale; neither defines the target
  animal, product size or requirements. Its part placement and callout attachment are illustrative.
- Wheel protection, stop controls, low battery placement and connection-loss stopping are
  design ideas/requirements. The images do not demonstrate that they work.
- Concept devices vary between boards. They are not a consistent CAD assembly, BOM or manufacturing package.
- Future owner-away scenes express a use-case hypothesis, not unattended-use readiness.

[Generation requests](generation-requests.json) preserve the prompts transcribed from the visible
tool calls. No deterministic seed or generator model version was returned/recorded; re-running
the text is not expected to reproduce identical pixels.

- [Sphero BOLT+](https://sphero.com/products/sphero-bolt-plus) — an existing spherical robot reference, not evidence of pet suitability or unattended safety.
